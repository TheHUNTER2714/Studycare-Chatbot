About StudyCare Chatbot

StudyCare is designed to provide emotional support during your academic journey. You can share your concerns, feelings, or ask for advice on managing stress, staying motivated, or balancing your studies.

While StudyCare offers support and suggestions, remember that for serious emotional or mental health concerns, it's important to reach out to a qualified professional or counselor.
